# AdmiScan Master's Programme Eligibility Research

AdmiScan analysed **4,009 verified, catalogue-ready Master's programmes** across the USA, UK and Germany to examine how undergraduate-background requirements are expressed at programme level.

Canonical report:
https://admiscan.com/research/ms/strong-profile-not-enough

Methodology:
https://admiscan.com/research/admiscan-research-standard

## Headline findings

| Country | Research programmes | Classifiable backgrounds | Explicit any-discipline | Related/relevant field | Explicit any-discipline share | Unknown share |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| USA | 2,715 | 2,587 | 124 | 2,362 | 4.79% | 4.71% |
| Germany | 729 | 637 | 7 | 586 | 1.10% | 12.62% |
| UK | 565 | 535 | 66 | 347 | 12.34% | 5.31% |

“Any discipline” and “related/relevant field” are different evidence classes and must not be merged into a single flexibility statistic.

Unknown evidence remains unknown. It is not treated as unrestricted eligibility.

This repository is a citation/distribution companion to the canonical AdmiScan report. The website remains the source of record.
