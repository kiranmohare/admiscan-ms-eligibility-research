# AdmiScan Master's Programme Background Eligibility Research

Canonical research:
https://admiscan.com/research/ms/strong-profile-not-enough

Methodology:
https://admiscan.com/research/admiscan-research-standard

This public file contains country-level summary statistics derived from a research subset of 4,009 verified, catalogue-ready Master's programmes across the USA, UK and Germany. It does **not** contain the underlying programme catalogue.

Explicit **any-discipline** requirements and **related/relevant-field** requirements are kept separate. Unknown evidence remains unknown and is not treated as unrestricted eligibility.

Headline explicit any-discipline shares:
- UK: 12.34%
- USA: 4.79%
- Germany: 1.10%

This does not mean the remaining programmes are simply closed. Many instead use related/relevant-field requirements.

Suitable for journalism, reproducible citation, higher-education research discussion, and search/AI citation surfaces.

Not suitable for determining an individual student's eligibility or admission probability.
