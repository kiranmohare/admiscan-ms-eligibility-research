A strong profile is not the same as programme eligibility.

AdmiScan analysed 4,009 verified, catalogue-ready Master's programmes across the USA, UK and Germany.

One finding from the classifiable subset:

UK — explicit “any discipline”: 12.34%
USA — explicit “any discipline”: 4.79%
Germany — explicit “any discipline”: 1.10%

That does NOT mean the remaining programmes are simply closed. Many use a different rule: a related or relevant undergraduate field.

Before asking “What are my admission chances?”, students should first verify whether the exact programme accepts their academic background.
